# Star-shooter

Star Shooter game made by me — a mobile-friendly, single-file arcade shmup ("Debris Run"). Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

Drag to steer, your ship autofires. Blast rocks and enemy squadrons, grab power-ups, take down mini-bosses, and survive the cosmos turning hostile as you go deeper.

## The Void Titan

Survive **3 minutes** and the field clears for a cinematic superboss encounter: **THE VOID TITAN**. It fights in three escalating phases — spiral bullet storms, sweeping laser beams, homing missile barrages, and a screen-crossing dash attack — each with its own epic battle music and a dramatic transformation sequence as its health drops. A threat meter on the right edge of the HUD tracks your progress toward the encounter, and the boss health bar shows phase pips so you always know how close you are to victory.
